Click a wall or floor to choose its finish.

Click any surface to paint it with this. Esc puts it down.

Finish

Hover a finish to try it on the map. None reveals the ground underneath. Double-click one to paint the whole room at once. Alt-click a surface to sample the finish already there.

Wall

Drag to lay a run; hold Ctrl to invert the tool. Hover a wall you already have to grab its handle: pull across it to move the whole run, or drag along it to lengthen it.

Doors and windows are not built here — they are things you own. Place one from your inventory onto a wall.

Fence

Drag to lay a run; hold Ctrl to invert the tool. Alt-click a fence to match its style. Removed pieces go back to your inventory.

Gates are not built here — drag one from your inventory onto a fence to replace that piece.

Style

Ground

Pick a ground and drag over the map. Edges blend into whatever they meet. Hold Alt to rub it out.

This map's tilesets have no ground to paint with. Add a corner wang set called Ground in Tiled.

Rooms

Click a room tile on the map to select its row. Use Floor or Walls to finish it, and Enclose to fill its wall gaps.

Add paints with the selected room; Remove returns floor to whatever the walls enclose. Hold Ctrl to invert the tool. Click to fill or drag square by square.
